# Piwowar 2007 reader fixture

Heather A. Piwowar, Roger S. Day, and Douglas B. Fridsma (2007), “Sharing
Detailed Research Data Is Associated with Increased Citation Rate,” PLOS ONE
2(3): e308. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0000308

The unmodified publisher PDF is distributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution License, as stated on its first page and the article page:
https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0000308

License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/

This five-page file exercises embedded CFF Type 1 fonts, including encodings
that the bounded text reader cannot fully interpret without an optional font
parser. Drawing and extracting canonical text are checked independently.
