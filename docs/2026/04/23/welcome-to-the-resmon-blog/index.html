Update

Welcome to the resmon blog

April 23, 2026

This is the first post on the resmon blog. Going forward, every release update to the resmon app will be mirrored here as its own post, including the full contents of that update’s update_<DATE>.md document, screen-recording GIFs of new features and bug fixes (where applicable), and any other context that helps users understand the change.

The blog is also surfaced inside the resmon app itself, on the About resmon → Blog tab. The in-app reader fetches this site’s feed at /feed.xml and renders the post list directly from it, so any post published here appears in the app on the next tab open without an app re-release.

Thanks for reading.
