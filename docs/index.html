Research moves fast. resmon does, too.

resmon is a free desktop app. It asks arXiv, PubMed, OpenAlex, CrossRef, and 21 more on a schedule or when you ask, keeps every paper it finds on your computer, and records what each source did on every run. This is its morning view, drawn from a real collection.

What every source did

A quiet morning and a broken search must never look the same. resmon records what every source did on every run, and says why a source returned nothing: it answered and had nothing, it could not be asked, or it never answered. What it does

Today
What is new since you last looked, on a map of everything you have collected. Papers sit by the wording they share, brighter the newer they are. The new ones are ringed and numbered, and each number is one entry in the list beside the map.
Watches
A search resmon runs on a schedule, keeping every paper it finds. A watch can follow words, a person’s new papers, or retractions and corrections on a person’s papers. The Calendar shows when each is due, and says so when nothing will run it.
Search
Ask the sources, or search what you already hold. A Deep Dive asks one source in depth and a Deep Sweep asks several at once. Before you ask, resmon lists each source’s limits: how it combines your terms, how finely it dates a paper, and whether it needs a key.
Library
A reading queue, your own files, and the passages you keep. Keep PDF, TXT, and MD files as unchanging copies in a folder you choose, read them in the app, and pin exact passages in Evidence projects. The Library is not yet available on Windows.
Ask
An assistant that reads your watches, runs, and papers. Through Claude Code or Codex under your own sign-in, or your own provider key. It uses the same tools an outside program can, and nothing is billed through resmon.
Health
Whether the watching itself is intact. The Source wall marks what every source did on each recent run. The Watchdog says what resmon can tell is wrong with its watching, and a check finds retractions and newer versions of papers you hold.
Outside the window
A command line, an MCP server, and a local HTTP API. The resmon command ships inside the app and installs from its menu. Tools that speak the Model Context Protocol can run watches and searches and read the collection.
Delivery and backup
Reports where you read them. Every run writes a report. A watch can email it, post it to a signed webhook, or put it in a folder or a feed on your own disk. Back up the database to a folder you keep.

Settings › Tutorials walks through each screen in the app, and the guide goes further.

On your computer

No account
There is nothing to sign up for, and no resmon server to sign in to.
Your collection
A database and a folder of reports on your own disk, with your watches, runs, and reading.
Your keys
Kept in your operating system’s keychain, never in the database, and sent only to the service they belong to.
No telemetry
resmon sends no usage data. A search sends its terms only to the sources it asks; summaries, delivery, and backup send only what you switch on, to where you point them.
